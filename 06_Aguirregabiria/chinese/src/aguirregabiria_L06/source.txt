k0270ca | INTRODUCTION
k2d3bb5 [4L] | Disentangling ⟪true dynamics⟫ —the causal effect of past decisions— from ⟪spurious dynamics⟫ arising from persistent unobserved heterogeneity (UH) is a fundamental challenge in the econometrics of dynamic models.
kd5805a | Challenges with short panels (Heckman, 1981):
k96a757 [2L] | ⟪Incidental Parameters Problem (IPP):⟫ Treating UH as fixed parameters implies inconsistent estimation of parameters of interest.
k19a281 [2L] | ⟪Initial Conditions Problem (ICP):⟫ There is no Nonparametric Identification of the distribution of UH and initial conditions.
k4d674a | Two alternative approaches to deal with the Nonparametric
k5f5a40 | No-Identification from the ICP:
k6c51cc | Random Effects (RE).
k3a1c16 | Fixed Effects (FE).
@page 3
k1dd1e6 | RANDOM EFFECTS (RE) vs. FIXED EFFECTS
kc061a9 | Random Effects (RE): ⟪Integrating out UH.⟫
k668b4f [2L] | We deal with the ICP by imposing parametric \& finite support restrictions on the joint distribution of UH and initial conditions.
k31599f | **Pros:** Full identification of structural parameters \& distribution of UH.
k0a9ed4 [2L] | **Cons:** The potential misspecification of parametric restrictions on UH can introduce substantial biases in the estimates of ”true dynamics”.
keb36e3 | ⟪Fixed Effects (FE):⟫ Differencing out UH.
k9ac10a [2L] | Focus on identification of structural parameters capturing ”true dynamics” and not on the identification of the distribution of UH.
ke500f5 | **Pros:** NP specification of UH. Robust identification of true dynamics.
ke4c271 [2L] | **Cons:** Distribution of UH is not fully identified. It limits the counterfactuals we can identify. ⟪(easy to deal with)⟫
kc438c9 | **Cons:** Not all dynamic models have consistent FE estimators.
@page 4
k5cba40 | FIXED EFFECTS IN STRUCTURAL DDC MODELS
k047fcb [2L] | Until recently, all applications of Structual DDC models use RE models to deal with UH.
k464e5f [2L] | The absence of applications using a FE approach was partly because of two common beliefs.
k7e25b1 [3L] | Belief that there are not consistent FE estimators in structural models where agents are forward-looking: ⟪problem with continuation⟫ ⟪values.⟫
k924a85 [3L] | Belief that, even if structural parameters are identified, we cannot identify ⟪Average Marginal Effects (AME)⟫ and other Counterfactuals as these depend on the distribution of the UH.
k1c62e0 | Recent developments have challenged these beliefs.
